From MIDI to printer music

1. Choose your printer and MIDI. U1 is the default. Klipper profiles make music through XY motion; supported Bambu Lab profiles use built-in music commands.

2. Adjust pitch range and tempo. For Klipper, speed selects the pitch range and motion ceiling. For Bambu Lab, Lower / Original / Higher changes pitch. Tempo scales the rhythm in either mode.

3. Generate and save G-code. Try the short test first. Bambu Lab transfer methods and native sound support depend on model and firmware; check profile notes.

Motion limits and timing are checked offline. Bambu uses native music commands, with no positioning or homing and a muted Z sound channel. V3 KE homes normally, then lifts to Z20 mm at 3 mm/s; slowing the stock initial Z hop requires the optional slow-Z configuration. K2 models home X/Y at normal machine limits. Stock homing behavior depends on firmware. Other Klipper profiles require prior homing, enabled motors, a cool printer and at least 20 mm nozzle-to-bed clearance. U1 automatic X/Y homing also requires this clearance. These checks are not physical or acoustic tests.
